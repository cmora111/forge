## Discovery Terminology

Forge Discovery titles are formal artifact names and SHALL be
capitalized when referenced by identifier and title.

Examples:

- FD-0001 — Engineering Tension
- FD-0002 — Investigation Pattern Recognition
- FD-0003 — Dependency Tracing
- FD-0004 — Bedrock Recognition

When referring to the engineering phenomenon described by a discovery,
normal English capitalization SHALL be used.

Examples:

- Engineering tension initiated the investigation.
- Continued dependency tracing exposed another unresolved dependency.
- Bedrock recognition determines when an investigation has reached its
  justified conclusion.

Capitalization at the beginning of a sentence follows normal English
grammar and SHALL NOT be interpreted as a formal artifact reference.
