# Decisions

Forge Decisions preserve justified engineering choices made among
multiple viable alternatives.

A Decision records:

- the engineering context;
- the alternatives considered;
- the selected alternative;
- the justification for the selection;
- the consequences of the decision; and
- the conditions under which the decision may be reconsidered.

A Decision does not establish engineering truth.

Discoveries preserve what engineering reality revealed.

Decisions preserve what engineers selected after considering the
available justified alternatives.
