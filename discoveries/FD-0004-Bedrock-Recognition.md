# FD-0004 — Bedrock Recognition

**Status:** Accepted
**Version:** 0.1.0

---

# Summary

Following the discovery of Dependency Tracing (FD-0003), the next
engineering question became how the Forge determines when an
engineering investigation has reached its justified conclusion.

This investigation discovered that engineering investigations reach
bedrock when continued disciplined investigation no longer reveals a
more fundamental engineering justification.

Bedrock Recognition therefore provides an engineering criterion for
concluding investigation without relying upon intuition, convenience,
or exhaustion.

---

# Context

FD-0003 established that explanatory engineering answers must be
inspected for unresolved engineering dependencies.

However, dependency tracing alone cannot determine when an engineering
investigation has reached its justified conclusion.

Repeated Forge investigations eventually reached points where further
questioning failed to expose more fundamental engineering
relationships.

This observation led to the investigation of Bedrock Recognition.

---

# Engineering Tension

Engineering investigations require a disciplined method for recognizing 
when continued investigation is no longer producing more fundamental 
engineering justification.

Without such a method, investigations risk either:

- stopping prematurely while unresolved engineering dependencies
  remain; or

- continuing indefinitely despite no longer producing more fundamental
  engineering justification.

The engineering tension therefore became:

> How does the Forge recognize that an engineering investigation has
> reached its justified conclusion?

---

# Forge Investigation

The Forge examined numerous investigations performed during the
development of the Forge–Stage Method.

Each investigation was traced until dependency inspection repeatedly
failed to expose a more fundamental engineering dependency.

The investigation repeatedly observed that some questions merely
restated previously established engineering principles rather than
revealing more fundamental justification.

This recurring condition became recognized as Bedrock Recognition.

---

# Discovery

>**Bedrock Recognition therefore determines when an engineering 
>investigation has reached its justified conclusion.**

Bedrock Recognition therefore determines when an engineering
investigation has reached its justified conclusion.

Bedrock Recognition does not declare an investigation permanently
complete.

It recognizes that the current investigation has reached the deepest
engineering justification presently revealed through disciplined
investigation.

---

# Bedrock Recognition

Bedrock Recognition is the disciplined observation that continued
dependency tracing no longer produces a more fundamental engineering
justification.

The absence of newly discovered fundamental dependencies indicates
that the current investigation has reached its justified conclusion.

Future engineering evidence MAY justify reopening the investigation.

Bedrock Recognition therefore concludes investigations without claiming
absolute or permanent truth.

---

# Bedrock

Bedrock was reached when the investigation determined that the
criterion for concluding an engineering investigation is itself an
engineering discovery rather than an engineering judgment.

No more fundamental criterion governing justified investigative
conclusion was discovered.

Dependency tracing determines how investigations advance.

Bedrock Recognition determines when investigations conclude.

---

# Rationale

Without Bedrock Recognition, engineering investigations possess no
disciplined criterion for determining when sufficient justification has
been achieved.

By recognizing that investigations conclude when continued dependency
tracing no longer reveals more fundamental engineering justification,
the Forge prevents both premature acceptance and endless investigation.

Bedrock Recognition therefore establishes disciplined engineering
closure.

---

# Implications

This discovery establishes that:

- investigations conclude through engineering evidence rather than
  intuition;
- dependency tracing alone does not determine investigative
  completion;
- bedrock is recognized rather than declared;
- future evidence may justify renewed investigation;
- engineering conclusions remain open to future validation.

This discovery provides the foundation for:

- future Forge Decisions;
- engineering closure;
- disciplined investigative completion.
