# FD-0027 — Governing Procedural Authority for Controlled Stage Admission

**Status:** Accepted
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Forge Question

What Procedural Authority governs Controlled Stage Admission?

---

# Context

FD-0026 — Procedural Verification Attribution established that procedural
verification is attributable to a governing Procedural Authority when
sufficient provenance establishes that the particular verification was
performed under a procedure legitimately authorized by that Procedural
Authority.

FD-0026 also established that procedural correctness, procedural execution,
and procedural mechanism identity do not independently establish Procedural
Authority or Procedural Authority attribution.

Controlled Stage Admission therefore requires more than evidence that an
admission procedure was executed correctly.

The admission must be governed by Procedural Authority legitimately applicable
to the admission operation.

FD-0026 left unresolved:

> What Procedural Authority governs Controlled Stage Admission?

That question is returned to the Forge here.

---

# Engineering Authority Does Not Automatically Govern Admission

Engineering Authority supplies substantive engineering determinations.

For Controlled Stage Admission, Engineering Authority may determine matters
such as:

- Stage responsibility;
- Stage scope;
- discharge conditions;
- lineage;
- predecessor relationship; and
- other substantive engineering judgments required by the admission
  procedure.

Possession of Engineering Authority does not automatically establish authority
to govern whether the procedural requirements for Controlled Stage Admission
have been satisfied.

Therefore:

> **Engineering Authority ≠ Automatically Admission Procedural Authority**

A single authority holder may occupy both Engineering Authority and Procedural
Authority roles.

Coincidence of identity does not establish that standing in one role creates
standing in the other.

---

# Admission Execution Does Not Establish Admission Authority

The person, mechanism, or process that performs an admission procedure does
not acquire Procedural Authority merely by executing that procedure.

Otherwise, the ability to perform Controlled Stage Admission would itself
create authority to govern Controlled Stage Admission.

That would allow execution to manufacture its own authority.

Therefore:

> **Admission Execution ≠ Admission Procedural Authority**

Execution may occur under Procedural Authority.

Execution is not independently the source of that authority.

---

# Admission Mechanism Does Not Govern Admission Merely by Operating

A procedural mechanism may implement some or all of the operations required
for Controlled Stage Admission.

Such a mechanism may include:

- a shell script;
- a graphical application;
- a workbook;
- a paper procedure;
- a field procedure;
- or another implementation capable of performing the governing procedure.

The mechanism may inspect evidence, perform checks, preserve records, prepare
an admission result, or perform an authorized publication operation.

Those capabilities do not make the mechanism the governing Procedural
Authority.

Therefore:

> **Admission Mechanism ≠ Admission Procedural Authority**

A mechanism may act through an authorized procedure without itself possessing
the authority under which that procedure operates.

---

# Governing Specification Does Not Automatically Govern by Authority

A specification may define the requirements for Controlled Stage Admission.

It may state:

- what evidence is required;
- what verification must occur;
- what conditions permit Admission Commit;
- what failure means;
- what provenance must be preserved; and
- what lifecycle result follows successful admission.

The existence of those requirements does not make the specification itself the
Procedural Authority.

A specification may express an authorized procedure.

It does not acquire authority merely because procedural requirements are
written within it.

Therefore:

> **Admission Specification ≠ Automatically Admission Procedural Authority**

A legitimate authority structure may give procedural force to requirements
expressed through a specification.

The specification and the authority under which it governs remain distinct.

---

# Artifact Custody Does Not Establish Admission Authority

A repository, filesystem, archive, database, records system, or other custody
mechanism may preserve authoritative Stage artifacts.

Such custody may establish or preserve matters such as:

- artifact identity;
- content;
- sequence;
- integrity;
- history; and
- authoritative location.

Control of that custody mechanism does not independently establish legitimate
Procedural Authority over Controlled Stage Admission.

The ability to place an artifact into authoritative custody is not itself the
authority to decide that the artifact is entitled to enter that custody.

Therefore:

> **Artifact Custody ≠ Admission Procedural Authority**

Custody may implement or preserve the result of an authorized admission.

Custody does not manufacture the authority governing that admission.

---

# Method Origination Does Not Automatically Establish Admission Authority

Origination of the Stage Method may be relevant to the history or constitution
of its governance.

Origination alone does not establish that the originator possesses permanent
Procedural Authority over every later Controlled Stage Admission.

Otherwise:

```text
Method Originator
        =
Permanent Procedural Authority
for Every Stage Admission
```

would follow without establishing the governance relationship that gives such
authority legitimate standing.

Therefore:

> **Method Origination ≠ Automatically Admission Procedural Authority**

Where method origination contributes to legitimate governance, that
relationship must be established through the applicable authority structure
rather than presumed from origination alone.

---

# Procedural Authority May Be Established Through Stage Governance

Controlled Stage Admission requires a governing Procedural Authority.

That authority need not arise from the admission mechanism itself.

Applicable Stage governance may establish a Procedural Authority role and the
scope of authority associated with that role.

The resulting relationship may take the form:

```text
Applicable Stage Governance
            |
            | establishes or recognizes
            v
Governing Procedural Authority
            |
            | governs
            v
Controlled Stage Admission
```

The governing Procedural Authority may then authorize a procedure through
which Controlled Stage Admission is performed.

This does not make the governance artifact, procedural specification, or
implementation mechanism automatically identical to the Procedural Authority.

The relevant requirement is that the authority holder possess legitimate
standing under the governance applicable to the Stage domain.

---

# Procedural Authority Need Not Be Universal Across Every Stage Domain

The Stage Method may be applied within different engineering domains,
organizations, projects, institutions, or other legitimate governance
contexts.

Nothing established here requires every implementation of the Stage Method to
share one universal human, institution, mechanism, or other authority holder
as its Procedural Authority.

The Stage Method may instead establish the authority requirements that must be
satisfied while allowing the applicable Stage domain to establish or recognize
the legitimate authority holder occupying the Procedural Authority role.

The relationship may therefore be:

```text
Stage Method
     |
     | establishes authority requirements
     v
Applicable Stage Governance
     |
     | establishes or recognizes
     v
Procedural Authority
     |
     | governs
     v
Particular Controlled Stage Admission
```

Therefore:

> **Stage Method Authority Requirements ≠ One Universal Procedural Authority**

Procedural Authority may be domain-relative while remaining subject to the
authority requirements of the Stage Method.

---

# Procedural Authority Cannot Be Established by Assertion Alone

An actor cannot establish legitimate Procedural Authority merely by asserting:

```text
I am the Procedural Authority.
```

Likewise, a mechanism, repository, specification, operator, or institution
cannot establish its own Procedural Authority merely by labeling itself as the
authority.

Otherwise, self-identification would be sufficient to create procedural
standing.

Therefore:

> **Procedural Authority Claim ≠ Procedural Authority Standing**

The claimed authority holder must possess legitimate standing under the
governance applicable to the Stage domain.

---

# Procedural Authority Must Be Applicable to Controlled Stage Admission

The existence of legitimate Procedural Authority somewhere within a Stage
domain does not establish that the authority governs every procedural
operation in that domain.

An authority holder may legitimately govern one operation without possessing
authority over another.

Therefore:

> **Domain Procedural Authority ≠ Authority Over Every Domain Operation**

For Controlled Stage Admission, the relevant Procedural Authority must possess
authority applicable specifically to the Controlled Stage Admission operation.

The required relationship is:

```text
Legitimate Procedural Authority
              +
Authority Applicable to
Controlled Stage Admission
              =
Governing Procedural Authority
for Controlled Stage Admission
```

The governing authority is therefore operation-relative as well as
domain-relative.

---

# Identity Form Does Not Determine Procedural Authority

Nothing established here requires the governing Procedural Authority to take
one particular identity form.

Depending upon the applicable legitimate governance, an authority holder might
potentially be represented by a person, institution, governance body,
designated role, or another legitimate authority form.

Identity form alone does not establish standing.

Therefore:

> **Procedural Authority Identity Form ≠ Procedural Authority Standing**

What matters for Controlled Stage Admission is whether the authority holder
possesses legitimate standing applicable to the admission operation.

---

# Governing Procedural Authority for Controlled Stage Admission

The Procedural Authority governing Controlled Stage Admission is the legitimate
authority holder whose standing under the governance applicable to the Stage
domain includes authority over the Controlled Stage Admission operation.

The governing Procedural Authority is therefore not identified merely by
asking:

```text
Who performed the admission?
```

or:

```text
Who owns the mechanism?
```

or:

```text
Who wrote the specification?
```

or:

```text
Who controls authoritative custody?
```

or:

```text
Who originated the Stage Method?
```

The relevant question is:

```text
What legitimate authority holder,
under the governance applicable to this Stage domain,
possesses Procedural Authority applicable to
this Controlled Stage Admission?
```

That authority holder is the governing Procedural Authority for the admission.

---

# Consequences for Controlled Stage Admission

Controlled Stage Admission must not infer its governing Procedural Authority
merely from:

- Engineering Authority identity;
- admission operator identity;
- procedural mechanism identity;
- specification authorship;
- repository ownership;
- artifact custody;
- implementation control;
- or Stage Method origination.

A particular admission must instead operate under Procedural Authority whose
legitimate standing is applicable to the Stage domain and to the Controlled
Stage Admission operation.

FD-0027 identifies the structural condition necessary to identify the
governing Procedural Authority.

It does not yet establish how a particular Stage domain proves that a claimed
authority holder possesses that standing.

---

# Does Not Establish

FD-0027 does not establish:

- the identity of the particular Procedural Authority governing the current
  Marlin Stage domain;
- that Engineering Authority automatically possesses Procedural Authority;
- that the same actor may not legitimately occupy both authority roles;
- that the operator of an admission procedure possesses Procedural Authority;
- that an admission mechanism possesses Procedural Authority;
- that a specification possesses Procedural Authority merely by expressing
  procedural requirements;
- that repository ownership or artifact custody establishes Procedural
  Authority;
- that the originator of the Stage Method possesses permanent Procedural
  Authority over every Stage domain;
- that every Stage domain must use the same Procedural Authority;
- that Procedural Authority must be a human individual;
- that a claimed authority holder may establish standing through
  self-declaration;
- what constitutes sufficient governance for establishing or recognizing a
  Procedural Authority holder;
- what evidence establishes that a particular authority holder possesses
  legitimate Procedural Authority over Controlled Stage Admission;
- what evidence must preserve that authority relationship for a particular
  admission;
- how an authorized admission procedure must be identified;
- how a procedural mechanism must be connected to that authorized procedure;
- or that the current Stage admission adapter already establishes the
  governing Procedural Authority.

# Open Dependencies

FD-0027 leaves unresolved:

1. What establishes that a particular authority holder possesses legitimate
   Procedural Authority over Controlled Stage Admission within a Stage domain?
2. What governance establishes or recognizes that authority holder?
3. What establishes that the authority granted by that governance applies
   specifically to Controlled Stage Admission?
4. What evidence is sufficient to preserve the identity and standing of the
   governing Procedural Authority for a particular admission?
5. What establishes that the governing Stage admission procedure is authorized
   by that Procedural Authority?
6. How must a particular procedural verification be connected to that
   authorized procedure?
7. When the same actor occupies Engineering Authority and Procedural Authority
   roles, what evidence sufficiently preserves the distinction between those
   roles?
8. What portion of this authority relationship must enter permanent Stage
   custody upon successful Admission Commit?
9. Whether the current Controlled Stage Admission specification sufficiently
   expresses the governing Procedural Authority requirement established by
   this finding.

These questions belong to subsequent Forge investigation, Anvil inspection, or
Stage operationalization according to whether they become blocking
dependencies.

# Forge Finding

> **The Procedural Authority governing Controlled Stage Admission is the legitimate authority holder whose standing under the governance applicable to the Stage domain includes authority over the Controlled Stage Admission operation.**
>
> **That authority is not established merely by Engineering Authority, execution of the admission procedure, control of the procedural mechanism, authorship of the governing specification, custody of Stage artifacts, or origination of the Stage Method.**
>
> **The Stage Method may establish the authority requirements governing Controlled Stage Admission without requiring one universal Procedural Authority for every Stage domain.**
>
> **A particular Controlled Stage Admission must be governed by Procedural Authority legitimately applicable to both the Stage domain and the admission operation being performed.**
>
> **A claim of Procedural Authority does not establish Procedural Authority standing; the authority holder must possess legitimate standing under the governance applicable to the Stage domain.**
>
> **The identity form of the Procedural Authority does not itself determine standing.**
