# FD-0036 — Bounded Constitutive Legitimacy of Canonical Method Governance

**Status:** Accepted
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Forge Question

What bounded basis can establish constitutive legitimacy for governance of the
canonical Forge–Stage Method without deriving Procedural Authority from
engineering truth, authorship, repository ownership, technical control, or
unlimited founder sovereignty?

---

# Context

FI-0006 — Ultimate Legitimacy of Applicable Governance was originally
deferred because the existence of a deeper legitimacy question had not been
demonstrated to block current engineering.

During the investigation of subordinate procedure authorization for Canonical
Discovery Acceptance, the narrower available authority paths were exhausted.
Historical practice, authorship, repository custody, epistemic survival,
operational Canonical Method Stewardship Authority scope alone, absence of
prohibition, candidate constitutional language, and prospective
self-constitution were each insufficient to establish the required authority
relationship.

At that point a required Method operation could no longer establish sufficient
legitimate Procedural Authority without resolving the constitutive legitimacy
of the governance under which that authority claimed standing.

The re-entry condition for FI-0006 was therefore satisfied by a demonstrated
blocking engineering dependency rather than by the mere existence of a deeper
question.

---

# Forge Investigation

## Strike 1 — Does Engineering Reality itself constitute Procedural Authority?

No.

Reality constrains what engineering claims may be true or justified. That does
not establish who may govern the canonical identity, artifacts, or lifecycle
of a voluntarily maintained Method.

```text
Engineering Reality
        ≠
Procedural Authority
```


## Strike 2 — Does authorship establish constitutive legitimacy?

No.

Authorship establishes a historical relationship to creation. It does not, by
itself, establish legitimate jurisdiction over later participants, derivative
Methods, engineering truth, or every future governance act.

```text
Authorship
        ≠
Unlimited Constitutive Authority
```


## Strike 3 — Does repository control establish constitutive legitimacy?

No.

The ability to edit, preserve, host, or control a repository establishes
capability and custody. It does not independently establish legitimate
Procedural Authority.

```text
Repository Control
        ≠
Governance Legitimacy
```


## Strike 4 — Does successful operation or epistemic survival establish it?

No.

A Method may work well, and a claim may survive disciplined inspection,
without thereby appointing an authority holder.

```text
Engineering or Epistemic Success
        ≠
Constitutive Standing
```


## Strike 5 — What bounded domain actually requires governance?

The preserved Method history establishes that the Forge–Stage Method emerged
from actual engineering activity conducted under bounded methodological
responsibility and subsequently became an identifiable canonical Method
through continuity of origin, identity, scope, lineage, methodological
practice, and preservation.

The relevant governed domain is therefore not Reality itself and not every
person who may use, reject, modify, or derive from the Method.

It is the deliberately maintained canonical identity of the Forge–Stage
Method.

```text
Actual engineering responsibility
        ↓
Method emergence
        ↓
continuity of origin / identity / scope / provenance
        ↓
Canonical Method Stewardship relationship
        ↓
bounded canonical domain
```


## Strike 6 — What makes governance of that bounded domain legitimate?

Within that bounded canonical domain, the applicable relationship is
stewardship of the maintained Method identity rather than coercive
jurisdiction over persons or engineering truth.

The legitimacy boundary is preserved by:

```text
bounded canonical-domain stewardship
+
continuity of origin and canonical identity
+
voluntary participation
+
freedom to reject, leave, modify, or fork
+
no asserted jurisdiction over outsiders
```


This does not convert creation or ownership into sovereignty. It identifies a
bounded relationship between the originating stewardship of the canonical
Method and governance of that maintained canonical identity.

## Strike 7 — Does constitutive standing create permanent sovereignty?

No.

Initial constitutive standing may ground constitution of governance within the
bounded canonical domain. Once governance is constituted, however, the
constituted governance state constrains subsequent exercise.

```text
Bounded Constitutive Standing
        ↓
Governance Constituted
        ↓
Constituted Governance
binds later exercise
```


Later amendment, delegation, authorization, succession, replacement, or other
authority transition must remain derivable from the authoritative state
applicable immediately before that transition.

Initial constitutive standing therefore does not create permanent
extragovernmental sovereignty.

## Strike 8 — Does the legitimacy question continue in infinite regress?

No blocking regress remains for the bounded engineering question.

The investigation does not claim a universal theory of political, moral, or
social authority. It establishes the domain-relative relationship required to
evaluate governance of this voluntarily maintained canonical Method while
preserving the boundary that no authority over outsiders or engineering truth
is thereby created.

A deeper normative question may remain philosophically possible, but no
further dependency has been demonstrated as necessary to answer the present
bounded engineering question.

# Forge Finding

> **The legitimacy of governance over the canonical Forge–Stage Method need
> not derive from coercive jurisdiction, universal ownership, technical
> control, authorship alone, or epistemic truth. The relevant canonical
> domain is a voluntarily maintained methodological identity that emerged
> from actual engineering activity under bounded methodological
> responsibility and remains continuously stewarded as an identifiable
> Method.**
>
> **For that bounded domain, continuity of origin and canonical identity,
> bounded domain stewardship, voluntary participation, freedom of exit or
> fork, and absence of asserted jurisdiction over outsiders provide a
> sufficient basis for constitutive standing over governance of the canonical
> Method itself.**
>
> **Once governance is constituted under that standing, the constituted
> governance state binds subsequent exercise. Initial constitutive standing
> does not create permanent extragovernmental sovereignty. Subsequent
> amendment, delegation, authorization, succession, or replacement must
> remain derivable from the governing authority state applicable immediately
> before the transition.**

# Does Not Establish

This discovery does not establish:

- authority over Reality or engineering truth;
- authority over independent users;
- authority over derivative Methods;
- authority from authorship alone;
- authority from ownership, repository custody, or technical control alone;
- unlimited or permanent founder sovereignty;
- the exact historical constituting act, sequence, or instant;
- that bounded constitutive standing was actually exercised historically;
- Canonical Method Stewardship Authority subordinate-procedure-authorizing
  scope;
- authorization of any particular subordinate procedure;
- authorization of `DISCOVERY-ACCEPTANCE-SPEC.md`;
- or that this Discovery creates the authority relationship it describes.
