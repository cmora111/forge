# FD-0038 — Direct Canonical Discovery Acceptance Authority

**Status:** Accepted
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Forge Question

Does the preserved Forge--Stage Method record establish that the continuing
Canonical Method Steward possesses bounded authority to perform a direct
Canonical Discovery Acceptance commit without first establishing or
authorizing a subordinate acceptance procedure?

---

# Context

FD-0036 establishes bounded constitutive legitimacy for governance of the
canonical Forge--Stage Method.

FD-0037 establishes that the bounded constitutive standing identified in
FD-0036 was historically exercised through a constituting sequence. The
resulting authority state includes continuing Canonical Method Stewardship
Authority operating within, and constrained by, constituted governance.

FD-0037 does not establish every substantive or procedural power of that
authority. It expressly leaves subordinate-procedure-authorizing scope
unresolved.

An earlier proposed bridge inferred direct acceptance authority from CMSA's
general jurisdiction over the canonical knowledge corpus. The Anvil rejected
that inference because subject-matter jurisdiction does not, by itself,
establish authority to perform a particular lifecycle operation.

The remaining bounded search therefore sought evidence of:

1. an express grant of direct acceptance authority;
2. preserved direct enactment practice grounded independently of this
   finding; or
3. another independently established authority relationship specifically
   permitting a Canonical Discovery Acceptance commit.

The search was confined to the preserved chronology, the Forge repository and
Git history, and the three originating Marlin conversations.

After the first Anvil inspection retained a continuity objection, the search
was narrowed further. It sought affirmative evidence that the identified
direct-acceptance role crossed the constituting boundary and was exercised
after the Method was operating under constituted governance.

---

# Forge Investigation

## Strike 1 — Does CMSA jurisdiction alone establish the power?

No.

FD-0037 establishes governance of canonical Method identity, lineage,
preservation, canonical artifacts, and the canonical knowledge corpus. It
also establishes bounded controlled evolution within existing authority.

That domain does not independently identify every permitted operation within
the domain.

```text
Authority over the canonical corpus
        !=
Authority to perform every corpus transition
```

The rejected inference therefore remains rejected.

## Strike 2 — Does the originating record identify an acceptance rule?

Yes.

On August 2, 2026, during completion of FD-0001, the working record states:

> A Forge Discovery becomes **Accepted** only after it has:
>
> 1. Been written.
> 2. Been inspected.
> 3. Survived the anvil.
> 4. Been supported by evidence.
> 5. Been accepted by the engineer responsible for the methodology.

The statement was contemporaneous with the first Canonical Discovery
Acceptance. It was not introduced later to solve the present authority gap.

The rule distinguishes epistemic conditions from the final acceptance act.
Writing, inspection, Anvil survival, and evidentiary support establish the
conditions preceding acceptance. Acceptance by the engineer responsible for
the Method performs the distinct final act.

## Strike 3 — Was the identified role merely hypothetical?

No.

Immediately after the rule was stated, Carlos Mora responded concerning
FD-0001:

> It has been accepted. ON to FD-0002 -Forge Triggers

Later that day, concerning FD-0003, Carlos Mora stated:

> It has been Accepted. now on to FD-0004

The surrounding record then treated each artifact as Accepted and continued
the Discovery sequence from that changed state.

These are preserved direct acceptance acts, not merely recommendations that
someone else perform an acceptance.

## Strike 4 — Does repository mutation alone establish authority?

No.

The repository preserves the resulting Accepted artifacts and repeated
acceptance commits authored by Carlos Mora. Those mutations corroborate that
canonical transitions occurred and identify the actor who preserved them.

Repository control, authorship, or the ability to change a status field does
not independently create Procedural Authority.

```text
Repository mutation
        !=
Authority for the mutation
```

The repository evidence is therefore corroborative. The authority basis must
remain independently grounded.

## Strike 5 — Is the responsible engineer independently identifiable?

Yes, within the bounded canonical domain.

The historical record identifies Carlos Mora as the person conducting the
originating CR-10S engineering work and exercising responsibility over the
methodological discipline used for that work.

FD-0036 independently establishes the bounded constitutive legitimacy of the
continuous originating stewardship relationship. FD-0037 independently
establishes that this standing was historically exercised and resulted in a
governed canonical Method with continuing CMSA.

The earlier acceptance rule's reference to "the engineer responsible for the
methodology" therefore identifies an historically evidenced role within that
same continuous originating stewardship relationship. It does not rely upon
repository ownership, authorship alone, later self-appointment, or this
Discovery's authority.

## Strike 6 — Is direct acceptance part of the preserved exercise?

Yes.

The constituting sequence established by FD-0037 includes the deliberate
maintenance of the Method as an identifiable canonical Method and the
establishment of principles and artifacts intended to govern its operation
and evolution.

Within that same historical sequence, the record preserves:

```text
epistemic conditions for Discovery acceptance
        +
an identified acceptance role
        +
express acceptance acts by Carlos Mora
        +
resulting canonical status changes
        +
continued treatment of the artifacts as Accepted
```

This evidence does not infer a new power from CMSA's general subject matter.
It identifies direct Canonical Discovery Acceptance as an operation actually
exercised within the originating stewardship relationship from which the
governed canonical corpus developed.

## Strike 7 — Did the identified role cross the constituting boundary?

Yes.

FD-0037 establishes that the continuous originating stewardship relationship
participated in the historical constituting sequence. It also establishes
that the resulting authority state includes continuing CMSA operating within,
and constrained by, the constituted governance.

The originating direct-acceptance role is therefore not connected to the
present merely by an absence of revocation. The authority relationship from
which the role arose is affirmatively carried through the constituting
sequence into the continuing governed authority state.

```text
originating stewardship relationship
        ↓
historical constituting exercise
        ↓
continuing bounded CMSA
```

This establishes continuity of the underlying authority relationship. It does
not establish every power of CMSA without separate evidence.

## Strike 8 — Does FD-0037's scope boundary defeat the evidence?

No.

FD-0037 states that its constituting-sequence finding does not, by itself,
establish every substantive or procedural CMSA power. That boundary prevents
deriving a specific power from FD-0037 alone.

The present finding does not derive the operation from FD-0037 alone. It uses
FD-0037 to establish the standing, historical exercise, and continuity of the
authority relationship. The originating record separately identifies the
specific direct-acceptance role and preserves exercises of that role.

```text
FD-0037 alone
        !=
Specific acceptance power

FD-0037 grounding and continuity
        +
contemporaneous role identification
        +
preserved direct acceptance acts
        =
evidence of bounded direct-acceptance authority
```

This preserves FD-0037's boundary while supplying the separate evidence it
requires for a particular power.

## Strike 9 — Was direct acceptance exercised after constitution?

Yes.

On September 13, 2026, FD-0036 and FD-0037 each passed through a deliberate
final lifecycle sequence after the Method was already operating under mature
constituted governance.

For FD-0036, the preserved record distinguishes final Anvil silence from the
separate authorized acceptance act. Carlos Mora then directed and completed
the transition from `Hold / 0.1.2` to `Accepted / 1.0.0`. The resulting
artifact, INDEX state, and repository commit were preserved.

The repository records:

```text
f88ec6798469addc7a72551645044703fe588591
Accept FD-0036 bounded constitutive legitimacy
Author: Carlos Mora
```

For FD-0037, the record again distinguishes final Anvil silence from the
authorized acceptance transition. Carlos Mora then completed the transition
from `Hold / 0.1.1` to `Accepted / 1.0.0`. The final artifact and INDEX were
inspected, and the transition was preserved in the repository.

The repository records:

```text
898e401164ae99c05b2ca383d879f63cf5524675
FD-0037 — Historical Constituting Exercise
Author: Carlos Mora
```

These are affirmative post-constitution exercises of the same kind of direct
acceptance act identified in the originating record. They are not offered as
authority merely because repository mutations occurred.

## Strike 10 — Does constituted governance preserve a distinct act?

Yes.

`KNOWLEDGE-LIFECYCLE.md` distinguishes Anvil eligibility from the authorized
procedural act that records the status and version transition. The Discovery
template likewise requires successful Anvil inspection followed by the
applicable acceptance transition.

Those artifacts do not independently identify the authority holder. They do
establish that constituted governance preserved the distinction between
epistemic inspection and a separate authorized lifecycle act.

```text
Anvil silence
        =
eligibility for transition

Anvil silence
        !=
the authorized transition act
```

The originating role evidence and the continuing stewardship evidence identify
the actor applicable to the direct acceptance acts examined here.

## Strike 11 — Is the present holder independently established?

Yes.

On September 15, 2026, a separate Forge investigation tested continuity of
the originating stewardship relationship into the present. It identified
Carlos Mora as the originating holder, traced the continuing authority state,
and examined affirmative evidence of his continued stewardship.

The resulting Present CMSA Holder Standing finding survived complete Anvil
inspection. That inspection expressly rejected authorship, repository
ownership, Git identity, technical control, founder status, and absence of a
competitor as independent sources of authority.

The surviving relationship is:

```text
Carlos Mora
        ↓
originating steward
        ↓
continuous originating stewardship relationship
        ↓
historical constituting exercise
        ↓
present holder of continuing bounded CMSA
        ↓
subject to constituted governance
```

Present-holder standing does not establish every CMSA power. It identifies
the present holder of the authority relationship whose specific
direct-acceptance operation is separately evidenced here.

## Strike 12 — Does historical practice alone create present authority?

No.

Repeated conduct does not become authority merely through repetition.

The relevant evidence is stronger than unexplained historical practice. The
record identifies the acceptance conditions, the actor responsible for the
final act, the originating exercises, continuity through constitution, the
present holder, the resulting canonical transitions, and deliberate
post-constitution exercises.

Continuity is also bounded by constituted governance. A later valid governing
restriction, reservation, revocation, succession, or replacement would
control. No recovered governing artifact was identified that transfers,
revokes, or prohibits the direct acceptance authority evidenced here.

The absence of such a restriction would not create authority. It matters only
because the affirmative authority relationship is independently evidenced.

## Strike 13 — Are the FD-0036 and FD-0037 transitions circular?

No.

FD-0036 and FD-0037 do not create the authority used to accept themselves.
The originating acceptance rule, identified acceptance role, and early direct
acts predate both Discoveries.

FD-0036 and FD-0037 establish the legitimacy, historical exercise, and
continuity of the underlying stewardship relationship. Their later acceptance
transitions provide evidence of post-constitution exercise; they are not the
origin of the specific power.

```text
later exercise of evidenced authority
        !=
creation of that authority by the later exercise
```

## Strike 14 — Does the bridge authorize an acceptance procedure?

No.

Performing a direct Canonical Discovery Acceptance commit and establishing a
reusable procedure governing future acceptance are distinct operations.

```text
Authority to perform a direct acceptance commit
        !=
Authority to establish or authorize
a subordinate acceptance procedure
```

The recovered record establishes the first relationship only.

It does not authorize `DISCOVERY-ACCEPTANCE-SPEC.md`, resolve FD-0035, or
establish general subordinate-procedure-authorizing authority.

## Strike 15 — May the authority accept an ineligible Discovery?

No.

The historical acceptance rule itself requires investigation, inspection,
Anvil survival, and evidentiary support before the responsible engineer's
acceptance act.

Direct acceptance authority supplies only the procedural side of the
transition. It cannot create epistemic eligibility or cure an unresolved
Anvil objection.

```text
Epistemic eligibility
        +
authorized direct acceptance commit
        =
candidate valid Canonical Discovery Acceptance
```

Neither condition substitutes for the other.

> CMSA jurisdiction over the canonical knowledge corpus. During the
> originating Discovery sequence, the Method identified Canonical Discovery
> Acceptance as requiring a final act by the engineer responsible for the

> Carlos Mora as the present holder of that continuing bounded authority.**

> identified steward. The contemporaneous acceptance rule, originating acts,
> constitutive continuity, present-holder evidence, and later direct acts
> supply the separate evidence of the particular power that FD-0037 does not

>
> **Accordingly, the recovered record supports a bounded continuing authority

> independently epistemically eligible and no applicable constituted
> governance prohibits, reserves, transfers, revokes, replaces, or otherwise

> to create, adopt, approve, select, or authorize a subordinate acceptance

# Does Not Establish

This discovery does not establish:

- authority from CMSA subject-matter jurisdiction alone;
- authority from authorship, repository ownership, custody, or control;
- that unexplained historical practice creates authority;
- unlimited or extragovernmental stewardship authority;
- authority over independent users, derivative Methods, or Reality;

- that Forge survival alone constitutes acceptance;
- that Anvil survival alone constitutes acceptance;

  `DISCOVERY-ACCEPTANCE-SPEC.md`;

# Consequences

An eligible Discovery need not remain in Hold solely because a subordinate
Canonical Discovery Acceptance procedure has not been authorized.

Where the authority relationship and epistemic predicates are sufficiently
demonstrated, the continuing Canonical Method Steward may perform a direct
acceptance commit without purporting to establish a reusable acceptance
procedure.

Each proposed transition still requires an inspectable record establishing:

- the exact Discovery and evaluated revision;
- the completed Forge and Anvil state;
- the evidence supporting epistemic eligibility;
- Carlos Mora's deliberate direct acceptance act;
- the present-holder and direct-authority basis relied upon;
- the canonical status and version transition;
- the resulting exact artifact state; and
- preservation of the transition in canonical history.

These evidentiary elements describe what must be inspectable in a claimed
direct act. They do not constitute or authorize a subordinate procedure.
