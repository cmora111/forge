# FI-0003 — Historical Canonical Acceptance Governance

**Status:** Hold
**Version:** 0.2.1

---
# Origin

**FD-0024** — Pre-Specification Canonical Acceptance Validity

**Deferred:** 2026-08-30

---

## Question

How should a claimed historical Canonical Discovery Acceptance that occurred
before formal Acceptance procedures existed be evidenced, evaluated, and
recognized under present governance?

---

## Why Deferred

The investigation of FD-0024 required determining whether the validity of
Canonical Discovery Acceptance depends upon the prior existence of a formal
Acceptance specification.

That investigation established that Acceptance validity does not originate in
the existence of the specification describing it.

A pre-specification Acceptance may therefore have been valid if the substantive
conditions necessary for valid Acceptance were independently satisfied at the
time of the Acceptance.

The investigation also established an important distinction between historical
validity and present demonstrability of that validity.

This exposed additional governance questions concerning:

- what evidence is sufficient to establish a claimed historical
  pre-specification Acceptance;
- how present governance should recognize a historically valid Acceptance;
- how claims whose historical validity cannot presently be demonstrated should
  be treated; and
- how claims of invalid or incomplete historical Acceptance should be handled.

Resolution of those questions was not demonstrated to be necessary to establish
whether pre-specification Acceptance could, in principle, have been valid.

They were therefore deferred rather than extending FD-0024 into a complete
retrospective-recognition procedure.

This investigation is preserved for future work and is not presently blocking
the engineering work that exposed it.

---

## Re-entry

This investigation was reopened on 2026-09-12 because its declared re-entry
condition was satisfied.

A present Forge–Stage engineering operation requires reliance upon existing
canonical governance in order to determine the procedural authority applicable
to approval of the Data Licensing and Provenance Specification.

The investigation therefore requires examination of the historical canonical
Acceptance acts upon which that governance depends. The approval history of
KNOWLEDGE-LIFECYCLE.md is preserved as a comparative historical witness and
does not, by its inclusion here, establish that Specification approval and
Canonical Discovery Acceptance possess identical governance requirements.

The existence of those historical status records does not itself establish
their historical validity.

Re-entry therefore establishes only that the deferred investigation has become
necessary. It does not establish the validity, authority, or canonical standing
of any historical act under examination.

## Preserved Findings

### Acceptance Validity and Specification Existence

The validity of Canonical Discovery Acceptance does not originate in the
existence of the specification that describes it.

Therefore:

    Acceptance Validity ≠ Acceptance Specification Existence

A pre-specification Acceptance may have been valid if the substantive
conditions necessary for valid Acceptance were independently satisfied at the
time of the Acceptance.

---

### Later Codification

A later specification may formalize and operationalize conditions that existed
before the specification.

It cannot create historical validity merely by declaring an earlier act
compliant.

Therefore:

    Later Codification ≠ Retroactive Authorization

---

### Recognition of Prior Validity

Present recognition of a prior Acceptance and the historical validity of that
Acceptance are distinct.

Therefore:

    Recognition of Prior Validity ≠ Creation of Prior Validity

A later recognition may establish that sufficient grounds presently exist to
treat an earlier Acceptance as historically valid.

The recognition does not create that historical validity.

---

### Historical Validity and Present Demonstrability

Whether an Acceptance was valid when performed and whether that validity can be
demonstrated now are distinct questions.

Therefore:

    Historical Validity ≠ Present Demonstrability

and:

    Failure to Demonstrate Validity ≠ Demonstration of Invalidity

Failure to establish a historical claim does not prove that the historical act
was invalid.

It may nevertheless prevent present governance from recognizing or relying
upon the claimed historical Acceptance.

---

### Corroborative and Constitutive Evidence

Evidence that merely demonstrates that a valid act occurred is distinct from an
evidentiary, memorializing, registration, publication, or canonical effect that
was itself a substantive condition of validity.

Therefore:

    Corroborative Evidence ≠ Constitutive Evidence

Subsequent loss or absence of corroborative evidence does not, by itself,
retroactively invalidate an otherwise valid Acceptance.

Where preservation, memorialization, registration, publication, or another
evidentiary or canonical effect was itself an applicable substantive condition
of valid Acceptance, failure of that condition is a failure of the Acceptance
itself rather than merely a later evidentiary deficiency.

---

### Historical Applicability

Requirements later formalized by a specification cannot automatically be
projected backward onto an earlier Acceptance.

Therefore:

    Later Formalization ≠ Historical Applicability

The substantive conditions applicable to the historical Acceptance must be
established from the conditions actually applicable at the time.

---

### Historical Claims

The assertion that a historical Acceptance occurred does not demonstrate that
the Acceptance was valid.

Therefore:

    Historical Claim ≠ Demonstrated Historical Validity

The absence of a prescribed evidence format at the historical time also does
not establish that evidence or other substantive conditions were unnecessary.

Therefore:

    Absence of Prescribed Evidence Format ≠ Absence of Evidence Requirement

---

### Incomplete Historical Acceptance

Present recognition cannot complete an Acceptance that was substantively
incomplete when originally performed.

Therefore:

    Recognition ≠ Completion of an Incomplete Historical Acceptance

Where historical Acceptance cannot be established as valid, a later valid
Acceptance may establish canonical standing prospectively.

Such later Acceptance does not retroactively convert an invalid or incomplete
historical act into a valid historical Acceptance.

---

### Bootstrap

The absence of an already-authoritative Acceptance specification does not waive
the substantive conditions required for valid Acceptance.

Therefore:

    Bootstrap ≠ Waiver

and:

    Bootstrap ≠ Self-Authorization

Authority and epistemic eligibility remain independently required.

Therefore:

    Authority ≠ Epistemic Eligibility

## Evidence Under Investigation

The present investigation begins with two concrete historical witnesses.

### FD-0007

Repository history preserves FD-0007 as a Discovery for which a transition
from Draft to Accepted is observable.

This evidence may establish that a canonical status transition was recorded.
It does not, by itself, establish the Procedural Authority under which that
transition was performed.

### KNOWLEDGE-LIFECYCLE.md

Repository history preserves a transition of KNOWLEDGE-LIFECYCLE.md from Draft
version 0.9.0 to Approved version 1.0.0 following Forge and Anvil review.

This evidence may establish that an approval act was recorded. It does not, by
itself, establish the Procedural Authority under which that act was performed.

### Repository Provenance

The preserved repository history identifies Carlos Mora as the actor performing
the examined canonical repository acts.

Authorship, commit identity, repository ownership, or technical ability to
perform a repository operation SHALL NOT, without an independently established
governing relationship, be treated as sufficient evidence of Procedural
Authority.

The investigation must therefore determine what authority relationship, if any,
was applicable to those acts when they occurred and what surviving evidence
establishes that relationship.

---

## Active Investigation

The re-entered investigation asks:

> What surviving historical evidence establishes the authority relationship
> under which the canonical acceptance of FD-0007 was performed, and is that
> evidence sufficient for present governance to recognize that Acceptance as
> historically valid?

Resolution of that question requires examination of the following preserved
questions:

1. What evidence is sufficient to demonstrate that a claimed historical
   pre-specification Canonical Discovery Acceptance was valid?

2. How should present governance evaluate evidence supporting such a claim?

3. By what mechanism, if any, should a historically valid Acceptance be
   formally recognized under present canonical governance?

4. What canonical effect should present recognition have?

5. How should a historical Acceptance claim be treated when available evidence
   is insufficient to establish either validity or invalidity?

6. How should present governance treat a historical Acceptance that can be
   demonstrated to have been invalid or substantively incomplete?

7. How should constitutive evidence requirements applicable at the historical
   time be distinguished from later-created procedural or evidentiary
   requirements?

8. What record should preserve the grounds upon which retrospective recognition
   is made?

These questions are preserved without assuming that retrospective recognition
must exist or that every historical Acceptance claim requires investigation.

---

## Re-entry Condition

Reopen this investigation only when a concrete Forge–Stage engineering
operation requires present reliance upon, recognition of, or disposition of a
claimed historical Canonical Discovery Acceptance whose validity must be
established from conditions predating the applicable formal Acceptance
procedure.

The mere existence of historical Discovery artifacts or possible historical
Acceptance claims is not, by itself, sufficient reason to reopen this
investigation.

---

### Re-entry Determination

**Re-entered:** 2026-09-12

The re-entry condition is satisfied.

The present approval investigation concerning the Data Licensing and
Provenance Specification requires reliance upon existing canonical governance
whose standing depends upon historical Canonical Discovery Acceptance. The
approval history of KNOWLEDGE-LIFECYCLE.md is retained as a comparative
historical witness.

FI-0003 is therefore reopened for active Forge investigation.
