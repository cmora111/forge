# FI-0006 — Ultimate Legitimacy of Applicable Governance

**Status:** Deferred
**Type:** Future Investigation

---

# Question

What ultimately establishes the legitimacy of the governance under which a
Procedural Authority obtains standing?

---

# Context

The investigation into the Procedural Authority governing Controlled Stage
Admission exposed a deeper authority question.

FD-0027 — Governing Procedural Authority for Controlled Stage Admission
established the structural requirement that a Controlled Stage Admission be
governed by a legitimate authority holder whose standing under governance
applicable to the Stage domain includes authority over the admission
operation.

The subsequent Forge investigation into the standing of a particular
Procedural Authority found that sufficient standing for the bounded
engineering operation may depend upon governance that establishes or
recognizes:

- the relevant authority holder;
- the Procedural Authority role;
- the scope of that authority;
- applicability of that authority to the Stage domain; and
- applicability of that authority to Controlled Stage Admission.

That finding exposes a deeper question:

```text
Procedural Authority
        |
        | obtains standing under
        v
Applicable Governance
        |
        | legitimacy established by
        v
?
```

The existence of this deeper question does not by itself demonstrate that the
question must be resolved before present Stage Method engineering may
continue.

---

# Reason for Deferral

The present engineering problem requires sufficient legitimate Procedural
Authority for the bounded Controlled Stage Admission operation.

It does not presently require a complete theory establishing the ultimate
constitutive legitimacy of every authority in the governance chain.

Following the dependency immediately would permit an investigation such as:

```text
What legitimizes the Procedural Authority?
        |
        v
Applicable Governance

What legitimizes that governance?
        |
        v
Constitutive Authority

What legitimizes that authority?
        |
        v
Prior Authority

What legitimizes that authority?
        |
        v
...
```

The existence of that regress is intellectually significant.

It has not been demonstrated to block the current engineering operation.

Therefore:

> **Interesting Dependency ≠ Blocking Dependency**

and:

> **Unresolved Question ≠ Required Present Investigation**

The question is preserved rather than discarded, but active investigation is
deferred.

---

# Engineering Boundary

For present Stage Method engineering, it is sufficient to investigate whether
an authority holder possesses legitimate Procedural Authority applicable to
the bounded operation being performed.

This Future Investigation does not require present engineering to establish:

- a universal theory of legitimate governance;
- the ultimate source of all Procedural Authority;
- the first constitutive authority in an authority chain;
- whether legitimate governance can ultimately bootstrap itself;
- whether every authority relationship requires a prior authority;
- a universal political or institutional theory of authority;
- a moral theory of authority;
- a metaphysical foundation for authority; or
- a final termination condition for every possible governance dependency
  chain.

Those questions may become relevant to this investigation if a demonstrated
engineering dependency requires them.

They are not presumed to be required merely because they can be asked.

---

# Re-Entry Condition

FI-0006 SHALL NOT return to active investigation merely because another
authority question refers to governance, legitimacy, standing, authorization,
or constitutive authority.

Re-entry requires a demonstrated engineering dependency.

Re-enter FI-0006 only if a Stage Method operation cannot establish sufficient
legitimate Procedural Authority without resolving the constitutive legitimacy
of the governance under which that authority claims standing.

The re-entry condition may be represented as:

```text
Unresolved Ultimate Governance Question
                +
Demonstrated Failure to Establish
Sufficient Authority for a Required
Stage Method Operation
                =
Candidate for FI-0006 Re-Entry
```

Without that demonstrated dependency:

```text
FI-0006
    =
DEFERRED
```

---

# Re-Entry Does Not Predetermine Scope

Satisfaction of the re-entry condition does not require every question related
to ultimate governance legitimacy to be investigated simultaneously.

If FI-0006 is re-entered, the investigation should begin with the narrowest
question demonstrated to block the engineering operation.

Further dependency tracing should occur only when required by evidence
encountered during that investigation.

Therefore:

> **Re-Entry ≠ Permission for Unbounded Dependency Expansion**

---

# Does Not Establish

FI-0006 does not establish:

- what ultimately makes governance legitimate;
- that an ultimate source of governance legitimacy exists;
- that every authority chain must terminate in a single constitutive
  authority;
- that authority may legitimately establish itself;
- that authority may not legitimately establish itself;
- that governance legitimacy requires a human authority;
- that governance legitimacy requires an institution;
- that the Stage Method itself is the ultimate source of Procedural Authority;
- that the creator of the Stage Method is the ultimate source of Procedural
  Authority;
- that the present Stage domain lacks legitimate governance;
- that the present Stage domain possesses legitimate governance;
- that the question is permanently irrelevant; or
- that the question must be answered before present engineering may continue.

# Disposition

**Deferred.**

The question is legitimate and preserved.

No demonstrated engineering dependency presently requires its resolution.

Present Stage Method engineering should continue at the bounded Procedural
Authority standing question without following the authority chain into
ultimate governance legitimacy.
