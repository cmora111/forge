# Prospective CR-10S Engineering Baseline Arrangement

## Candidate Status

This document is a non-operative candidate instrument.

Its creation, inspection, preservation, download, revision, or repository
placement does not constitute this arrangement, authorize any procedure, or
produce any engineering baseline.

The arrangement becomes operative only through the deliberate constitutive
act specified in this document.

## Purpose

This candidate defines a possible prospective arrangement for establishing
and maintaining an engineering baseline for Carlos Mora's future CR-10S work.

It does not amend, continue, repair, replace, interpret, or derive authority
from the existing Forge--Stage Method.

## Governed Domain

If constituted, this arrangement governs only:

- future CR-10S engineering work performed by Carlos Mora; and
- artifacts that Carlos deliberately originates within, or prospectively
  submits to, this arrangement.

An artifact is not governed merely because it is inspected, cited, copied,
stored, downloaded, committed, or historically related to the CR-10S work.

Submission must be deliberate and must identify the artifact and the
prospective effect of its submission.

## Grounding

The proposed arrangement is grounded only in Carlos Mora's responsibility for
his own future engineering work and his voluntary submission of that work to
the governed domain.

Its authority is not grounded in authorship, ownership, repository control,
historical practice, existing CMSA, or Anvil inspection.

Nothing in this document creates authority over another person or over work
that another person has not voluntarily submitted.

## Constitutive Act

Constitution requires a deliberate act by Carlos Mora that:

1. identifies this candidate by its exact filename and revision;
2. identifies the exact bytes being constituted;
3. states an unambiguous intent to constitute the arrangement;
4. records a definite effective timestamp; and
5. creates and preserves a completed execution record.

No implied act, inspection result, download, file preservation, repository
commit, or later conduct may substitute for these requirements.

## Proposed Initial Holder

If the constitutive act occurs, Carlos Mora becomes the initial authorized
holder only for the authority expressly constituted by this arrangement.

Candidate status alone creates no holder and grants no authority.

## Constitutive Limits

The arrangement, if constituted, does not authorize the holder to:

- amend or govern the existing Forge--Stage Method;
- Freeze, admit, restore, continue, succeed, or transition historical Stage 3;
- convert historical acceptance into present authority;
- manufacture evidence, interpretive standing, or historical authority;
- impose governance on another person or unsubmitted work;
- enlarge the governed domain by implication;
- give retroactive effect to a baseline or governance act; or
- treat Anvil inspection as authorization or enactment.

Historical artifacts may be considered as evidence. Their use as evidence
does not place them under this arrangement or change their historical status.

## Prospective Baseline Origination

If this arrangement is constituted, it directly authorizes the following
procedure within the governed domain.

### Initiation

The authorized holder identifies:

- the candidate baseline artifact;
- the work or decision the baseline will govern;
- the evidence offered in support;
- known gaps, conflicts, and uncertainties; and
- the proposed prospective effective boundary.

### Inspection

The candidate and its supporting evidence are submitted to a bounded Anvil
inspection.

The Anvil tests whether the evidence is sufficient for the stated baseline,
whether material gaps remain, and whether the proposed result stays within the
governed domain and constituted authority.

Anvil inspection is epistemic only. An Anvil result neither originates the
baseline nor performs a governance act.

### Holder Determination

After inspection, the authorized holder makes and preserves one of two
determinations.

#### Sufficient Evidence

If the evidence is sufficient, the holder may deliberately establish an
Established Prospective Baseline with a definite prospective effective
boundary.

The record must identify the exact baseline artifact, supporting evidence,
inspection result, effective timestamp, and deliberate establishment act.

#### Insufficient Evidence

If the evidence is insufficient, no baseline is established.

The record must preserve the unresolved gaps, conflicts, or uncertainties.
Refusal does not authorize a weaker baseline by implication.

## Baseline Effect

An Established Prospective Baseline governs only future work inside this
arrangement from its recorded effective boundary.

It does not become historical Stage 3, alter Stage 3, or inherit any Stage
lifecycle status. The term `ACTIVE` is not used as a baseline status under this
arrangement.

## Bounded Maintenance

Ordinary maintenance is permitted only when a proposed change remains within
both the established governed domain and authority already constituted.

A bounded maintenance change must:

- preserve every constitutive limit;
- avoid enlarging the holder's powers;
- leave the grounding, governed domain, holder, and arrangement identity
  unchanged;
- avoid acquiring authority over historical artifacts or operations;
- preserve voluntary participation and freedom of exit;
- create no retroactive effect; and
- be deliberately enacted and preserved by the authorized holder after Anvil
  inspection.

The required sequence is:

1. the authorized holder proposes a bounded change;
2. the Anvil inspects the proposal;
3. the authorized holder deliberately enacts the inspected change; and
4. the change takes effect only at its recorded prospective boundary.

Anvil survival is not governance enactment.

A proposed change to the grounding, governed domain, holder, arrangement
identity, constitutive authority, external-jurisdiction boundary, or
non-retroactivity boundary is outside ordinary maintenance. Such a change
requires separately established constitutive standing.

## Voluntary Exit

Carlos Mora may stop submitting future work to this arrangement.

Exit does not retroactively alter preserved records or established baseline
effects that occurred before the exit boundary.

## Historical Boundary

Historical Stage 3 and any baseline established under this arrangement are
distinct objects with distinct identities and authority chains.

No similarity of subject matter, evidence, files, or engineering conclusions
merges those objects or transfers authority between them.
