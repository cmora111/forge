## 1. Purpose and evidentiary rules

Trace the CDA Draft's requirements to their proposed foundations,
distinguishing **textual correspondence**, **normative standing**,
**applicability to a particular Discovery**, and **proof of actual execution**.
A source's presence in this matrix is not proof that it is Accepted or
independently authoritative. A Draft does not authorize itself; a Hold finding
is not promoted by citation; an archive digest does not prove current
working-tree identity; this trace does not establish review completion,
authorization, or any Discovery's eligibility or acceptance.

Source references below are repository-relative where available. Line ranges
refer to the 886-line archived CDA Draft; other sources are identified by title
and relevant section because their exact current working-tree paths, revisions,
and line offsets have not all been independently bound in this workpiece.
Statements marked **candidate** are relationships to test, not operative
grants.

**Disposition terms:** TEXT = wording located in CDA; CONCEPT = corroborating
investigative concept located; STANDING OPEN = the source's authority for this
use is not demonstrated; APPLICATION OPEN = evidence for a specific transition
is absent; AUTHORIZATION OPEN = power or actual act not demonstrated.

## 4. Dependency-specific standing tests

### D-EL — Epistemic eligibility

Candidate source chain: F17 → F18 → F19 → applicable governed epistemic
predicates and actual Eligibility Case. F17–F19 examined Library copies remain
Hold v0.1.0. Their conceptual statements are mutually consistent, but neither
Hold status nor their use by CDA proves their governing standing. Determine
whether independently operative governance supplies the predicates and permits
the proposed reliance; bind the exact evaluated Discovery state and preserved
evidence. Do not equate lack of demonstration with proven ineligibility.

### D-PA — Procedural Authority for an individual acceptance

Candidate chain: adopted governance → legitimately constituted and currently
effective holder → scope over canonical Discovery admission and particular
action → deliberate authorized transition. F36/F37 support bounded CMSA in the
archived snapshot, but do not establish every CMSA power. A holder's identity
or stewardship title alone cannot replace the operative grant, scope, effective
time, or actual act. For each acceptance, preserve those facts through commit.

### D-PROC — Authorization of the CDA specification itself

This is distinct from D-PA. F35 is Hold in the inspected snapshot and
distinguishes operation authority from procedure authorization. AR reports a
later bounded derivative-capacity finding and Anvil outcomes, but the full
original source/inspection record remains partly unverified; AR itself is Hold
and records **no authorization performed**. A prospective independently
inspected derivation or recovery of original evidence, an identified current
holder and authority, a determinate exact CDA object, applicable review
completion, and an actual deliberate act remain to be established. Do not infer
authorization from a digest, Draft text, repository placement, or this trace.

### D-CORPUS — Canonical representation and atomicity

CDA requires authoritative corpus structures to agree at commit but delegates
identification of those structures to applicable canonical-corpus
specifications. Locate exact operative registry/index/manifest and
status/version semantics, and test whether a proposed adapter can implement
atomic effects without creating its own governance. AD permits multiple
implementation media but grants no new authority.

### D-REVIEW — Review and canonical standing of the specification

The CDA Version History expressly reserves Stage review and further governance.
The present substantive examination is not an authorized Stage review. Recover
the original decision defining the review or prospectively establish a
legitimate route; identify reviewer/holder, applicable requirement, examined
exact object, actual examination, outcome and completion evidence. Separately
determine any required status change and authorization act. Do not backdate
either event.
