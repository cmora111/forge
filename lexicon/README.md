# The Forge

> *The research arm of the Stage Method.*
> *The Forge's responsibility isn't to answer questions—it's to conduct disciplined inquiry.*

---

## Purpose

The Forge exists to investigate foundational questions that arise during the development of the Stage Method.

Unlike specifications or implementations, the Forge does not seek immediate solutions. Its purpose is to discover underlying principles by following dependency chains wherever they lead.

Questions may remain open for days, months, or years.

The objective is not to reach conclusions quickly, but to reach conclusions that are justified.

---

## What Belongs in the Forge?

The Forge contains **inquiries**, not answers.

An inquiry documents:

- a foundational question,
- the motivation for asking it,
- observations,
- hypotheses,
- relationships to other inquiries,
- and, eventually, any conclusions that emerge.

Each inquiry is independent and may reference other inquiries as dependencies become apparent.

---

## Inquiry Status

An inquiry progresses through stages of maturity.

```
UNDER INVESTIGATION
        ↓
DISCOVERED
        ↓
CONSTITUTIONAL (if warranted)
```

Not every inquiry will become constitutional.

Some may remain unresolved indefinitely.

Others may be superseded by later discoveries.

---

## Relationship to the Stage Method

The Forge is intentionally separated from:

- **Specifications** — which define behavior.
- **Reference Implementations** — which demonstrate behavior.
- **Adapters** — which realize behavior within projects.

The Forge asks the questions that those artifacts depend upon.

---

## Current Inquiries

- INQ-0001 — What gives the originating source the authority to require anything at all?
- INQ-0002 — Where should meaning reside: within an identifier or within its surrounding context?

---

## Philosophy

The Forge follows one principle above all others:

> **Do not invent principles. Discover them.**

Every inquiry begins with an engineering problem.

The Forge follows the dependency chain until the underlying question reveals itself.

Only after sufficient evidence exists should a principle become part of the Stage Method.

Until then, it remains an inquiry.

---

*"The Forge does not manufacture truth; it refines understanding."*
