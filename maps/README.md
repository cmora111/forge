# Forge Maps

Forge Maps preserve the architectural relationships among independently
accepted Forge artifacts.

Unlike Discoveries, Maps do not establish engineering truth.

Unlike Constitutional Laws, Maps do not govern engineering behavior.

Unlike Specifications, Maps do not prescribe engineering procedure.

Instead, Maps illustrate how accepted Forge artifacts interact to form
the architecture of the Forge–Stage Method.

A Forge Map introduces no independent engineering authority.

Its authority is entirely derived from the accepted artifacts whose
relationships it depicts.

Maps SHALL remain faithful to the artifacts they represent and SHALL
NOT introduce concepts that have not been independently established
through accepted Forge artifacts.

## Current Maps

- **The Forge Feedback Loop** — Operational architecture of the
  Forge–Stage Method derived from the first four accepted Forge
  Discoveries.

## Relationship to Other Artifacts

Forge Maps occupy a unique role within the repository.

- **Constitutional Laws** govern engineering.
- **Discoveries** preserve engineering truths.
- **Specifications** define engineering procedure.
- **Maps** illustrate architectural relationships among accepted
  artifacts.

Maps exist to improve understanding.

They do not create authority.
