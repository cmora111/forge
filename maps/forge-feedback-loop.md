# The Forge Feedback Loop
## Operational Architecture

**Artifact:** Forge Map
**Status:** Accepted
**Version:** 0.1.0

---

# Purpose

Engineering Reality is the environment in which the Forge operates. 
Engineering Tension is the operational entry point of every Forge 
investigation.

The Forge Feedback Loop is an architectural reference map that depicts
the operational execution model of the Forge–Stage Method.

It illustrates how independently accepted Forge Discoveries govern the
progression of a disciplined engineering investigation.

This map introduces no independent engineering authority.

Its authority is entirely derived from the accepted Forge Discoveries
whose operational relationships it depicts.

---

# Scope

This map represents the investigative lifecycle established by the
first four accepted Forge Discoveries:

- FD-0001 — Engineering Tension
- FD-0002 — Investigation Pattern Recognition
- FD-0003 — Dependency Tracing
- FD-0004 — Bedrock Recognition

No additional concepts are introduced.

# Interpretation

The Forge Feedback Loop represents the operational execution model of
the Forge–Stage Method.

Each phase of the loop is governed by an independently accepted Forge
Discovery.

The loop begins when engineering tension is recognized.

Engineering tension originates through observation of engineering
reality and therefore serves as the operational entry point of every
Forge investigation.

When Bedrock Recognition concludes an investigation, the engineer
returns to engineering work.

Subsequent observation of engineering reality may reveal additional
engineering tension, initiating a new investigative cycle.

The Forge therefore does not generate engineering work.

Engineering reality generates engineering work.

The Forge provides the disciplined method by which that work is
investigated.

Each completed investigation changes the engineer's understanding, 
enabling future engineering tension to be recognized with greater clarity.

The Forge Feedback Loop was not designed independently.

first four accepted Forge Discoveries.

Its validity therefore depends upon the continued validity of those
discoveries.

Future accepted discoveries may extend this map but SHALL NOT contradict
its accepted relationships without reopening the supporting
discoveries.
