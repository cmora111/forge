# Step 2 — Authority relationship test

Required chain:

1. Legitimately applicable, constituted governance.
2. An independently established grant or retained discretion to authorize
   binding subordinate procedures, with identified holder and scope.
3. A sufficiently determinate procedure and a deliberate authorization act.
4. Applicability to the particular governed operation, prospectively only.

FD-0036/37 support bounded governance and continuing CMSA, but FD-0037
explicitly reserves step 2. FD-0030 and FD-0035 prohibit substituting
jurisdiction over an operation for power over its procedure. The
retrospective record asserts a derivative capacity and reports inspection
silence, but neither assertion nor reported silence independently supplies
its originating grant. The proposal does not perform step 3. CDA's Draft
status and the unresolved Stage review also cannot be silently converted
into authorization. The four-link chain is NOT established on verified
primary evidence.

# Step 3 — New Anvil inspection

**Inspection target:** The claim that the verified September 17 sources,
plus the retrospective record, presently suffice to establish CMSA's
binding subordinate-procedure-authorizing power and permit CDA or Freeze
authorization.

**Strike A — Derivative-authority tautology: RETAINED.** The claimed
capacity is inferred from bounded canonical stewardship, while FD-0037
expressly excludes establishing that specific power. No independently
established grant, retention, or valid delegation is identified. The
retrospective statement cannot be its own authority source.

**Strike B — Necessity as authority: RETAINED.** A procedure may be
necessary to administer a governed operation without the operation's
holder having authority to prescribe its binding means. FD-0030:224-248
explicitly preserves that distinction.

**Strike C — Reported Anvil silence as original proof: RETAINED.** The
retrospective record reports SILENT results but disclaims original-source
verification. A reconstructed verdict cannot certify the missing
original derivation, inspection scope, objections, and chronology.

**Strike D — Proposal as executed act: RETAINED for any assertion of
current CDA authorization.** The instrument identifies itself as
unexecuted and records no deliberate confirmation, effective event, or
preservation of an executed act.

**Strike E — Historical backfill: RETAINED for any retrospective effect.**
Even a future valid authorization would not establish that Stage 3 or
previous CDA operations occurred under it. Legacy evidence needs its own
independently established interpretive standing.

**Verdict: NOT SILENT.** This is the present inspection of the stated
claim, not a representation that the historical Anvil's reported verdicts
were different. The minimum-conditions analysis itself can remain a
working Forge finding; the unproved assertion of present CMSA power and
any claimed completed authorization cannot survive this inspection.
