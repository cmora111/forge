## Investigation question

Which eligibility requirements in the CDA Draft are supported by text with
independently verified governing standing, which have support only as
investigative findings, and which remain unlocated in the examined source set?

## Evidence method and limits

Directly inspect the archived text; distinguish (a) a source's embedded status,
(b) its engineering proposition, (c) its demonstrated adoption/authority
provenance, and (d) applicability to a Discovery acceptance. An embedded
`Accepted` label is not proof of an adoption act, continuing force, or absence
of supersession. A Hold finding may explain a design without itself becoming
binding. This pass searched the archived lifecycle, Forge constitution and
constitution directory, the Discovery index and relevant Discovery files; it is
**not** a complete proof that no other governance exists in any other
repository or current tree.

## A-01 — Exact lifecycle clauses and standing

Source: `forge/specifications/KNOWLEDGE-LIFECYCLE.md`, embedded **Accepted
v1.0.0** in the inspected ZIP. Exact excerpts below are short identifying
passages; section names and source text must be checked against the archived
object when relied upon.

**Record 1**

**Source clause:**

Lifecycle Map, closing rule

**Exact operative wording or decisive excerpt:**

“No artifact SHALL be promoted to a later stage without satisfying the
responsibility of every stage required for that promotion.”

**Supported scope / limit:**

A general lifecycle promotion condition; it does not identify
Discovery-specific predicates or a canonical acceptance procedure.

**Record 2**

**Source clause:**

Authority Model

**Exact operative wording or decisive excerpt:**

“Engineering justification does not automatically create procedural authority.”
/ “Procedural adoption does not make an engineering claim true.”

**Supported scope / limit:**

Independent engineering/procedural chains; neither chain substitutes for the
other.

**Record 3**

**Source clause:**

Discovery

**Exact operative wording or decisive excerpt:**

“It records the Question, relevant evidence, explanatory answer, limitations,
and unresolved dependencies belonging outside its responsibility.”

**Supported scope / limit:**

Defines Discovery content and descriptive scope; no Eligibility Case schema or
Hold→Accepted act.

**Record 4**

**Source clause:**

Procedural Adoption

**Exact operative wording or decisive excerpt:**

“Procedural Adoption is the governing act by which a justified Candidate
Principle is granted normative force within the Forge.”

**Supported scope / limit:**

Expressly addresses Candidate Principle adoption, **not** automatically the
acceptance of a Discovery. Its foundation/scope/authorized-adopter checks
cannot simply be transplanted as CDA-specific procedure.

**Record 5**

**Source clause:**

Dependency Model

**Exact operative wording or decisive excerpt:**

“No artifact acquires engineering or procedural authority merely because it
depends upon another artifact.”

**Supported scope / limit:**

Citation and dependency do not confer authority.

**Record 6**

**Source clause:**

Approval Gate

**Exact operative wording or decisive excerpt:**

“Completion of an Anvil revision pass does not approve this document.” / “The
Anvil's silence establishes engineering eligibility for approval. It does not
itself perform the procedural status transition.”

**Supported scope / limit:**

Explicitly document-specific (“this document”); not a universal Discovery
acceptance gate.

**Standing verification:** Embedded status/version and clauses verified against
the named archived bytes. This pass did **not** locate and independently
validate the lifecycle's original approval/adoption act, complete provenance,
or current supersession state. Accordingly, replace “independently established
governing foundation” with **“recorded Accepted lifecycle text supplying a
general conceptual and putatively governing foundation, subject to
provenance/applicability verification.”** A-01's exact-clause requirement is
addressed; its standing-evidence requirement remains OPEN.
