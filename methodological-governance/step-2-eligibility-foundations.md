## Key distinction

The lifecycle is an independently recorded Accepted source for the **general
dual-chain discipline**. It does not, by itself, enact all the detailed
Discovery eligibility predicates developed in FD-0017–0019. The Hold findings
can inform a Draft's engineering design without automatically supplying
operative governance. Conversely, Hold status alone does not prove the
technical concepts wrong or bar examination of them.

An Eligibility Case cannot be inferred from a document's presence, a status
label, Forge examination, Anvil silence, or the authority of a steward. Its
applicable criteria, evidence, evaluated state and conclusion must be
inspectable under independently established applicable governance.

## Open evidence requests

1. Locate and inspect any adopted, Discovery-specific eligibility and
   acceptance governance beyond the lifecycle, including its adoption record,
   current applicability and supersession history.
2. Establish the exact governed epistemic predicates and thresholds applicable
   to each relevant Discovery class, without inventing a universal set.
3. Identify the authorized basis, form, and reviewer/determiner (if prescribed)
   for an Eligibility Case; bind evidence to a specific evaluated state.
4. Separately prove CDA procedure authorization and the Stage review/approval
   requirements. Neither is supplied by eligibility evidence.
5. For a proposed acceptance, inspect its actual eligibility evidence, source
   state, actor, authority scope and temporal effectiveness; do not assert an
   event occurred merely because its prerequisites are described.
