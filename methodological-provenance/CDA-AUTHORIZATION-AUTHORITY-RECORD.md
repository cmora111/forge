# Chronological Investigation and Inspection Record

## 1. Constitutive foundations

FD-0036 establishes bounded constitutive legitimacy for stewardship of the
voluntarily maintained canonical Method. It rejects authorship, ownership,
control, and unlimited founder sovereignty as independent authority grants.

FD-0037 establishes the historical constituting sequence and continuing,
bounded CMSA, constrained by constituted governance. It does not itself

authorization of the CDA specification.

Both Discoveries are Accepted v1.0.0 in the inspected Forge snapshot.

## 2. Subordinate procedure-authorizing scope

Retained Forge finding: where legitimately constituted CMSA possesses
substantive governance jurisdiction and bounded governance-forming capacity,
it possesses derivative capacity to authorize subordinate procedures needed
to exercise governance within that existing canonical domain. This cannot
expand jurisdiction, override superior governance, or bootstrap authority.
CDA concerns controlled entry into the canonical Discovery corpus, but
capacity to authorize CDA is not actual authorization of its specification.

Reported Anvil result: SILENT for this bounded scope finding.

## 3. Requirements for an actual authorization act

Retained Forge finding: an independently establishable deliberate exercise
of applicable procedure-authorizing authority must identify a sufficiently
determinate procedure and bounded scope. Inspectable evidence must establish
holder, authority basis, action, procedure identity, scope, resulting
standing, and provenance or ordering. Authorship, Forge findings, Anvil
survival, status text, repository placement, implementation, and Git commits
do not independently constitute authorization. No retroactive effect.

Reported Anvil result: examined as part of the later combined-chain Anvil
inspection; no separate earlier Anvil pass is claimed.

## 4. Authorized form and avoidance of regress

Retained Forge finding: an authorized holder may directly exercise existing
procedure-authorizing authority where constituted governance affords
appropriate discretion. Requiring a prior subordinate authorization
procedure for every authorization act would create regress. Governance
silence alone does not grant discretion; no prescribed form or historical
CDA authorization act was established by this investigation.

Reported Anvil result: examined in the combined-chain inspection.

## 5. Bounded representational discretion

Retained Forge finding: CMSA may choose an incidental representational form
for an existing authorization exercise where no superior exclusive or
conflicting form governs. That form cannot change holder, source, scope, or
substantive authorization predicates. Representation cannot create authority.

Reported Anvil result: examined in the combined-chain inspection.
