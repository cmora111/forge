## Question and proof standard

Does constituted Forge–Stage governance independently establish that continuing
CMSA may determine binding means of exercising its canonical jurisdiction,
including authorizing a subordinate procedure? Separate (a) operative content,
(b) actual adoption and temporal standing, and (c) applicability to CMSA and
the particular procedure. An artifact's title, filename, presence, or
self-declared status is evidence of its representation, not independently
conclusive proof of the act that gave it standing.

   holder, scope, date/effectiveness, and supersession history.

   Accepted Knowledge Lifecycle, Stage laws and lifecycle specification;
   resolve `CONSTITUTION.md`'s LAW-0001 reference mismatch without rewriting
   historical records.

   expressly delegated procedural authority, a specified amendment mechanism,
   or none. Do not conflate these possibilities.
4. For each proposed procedure, identify exact text/version, operation, domain,
   superior constraints, holder, deliberate authorization act, applicability,
   effective date, and evidence preserving the relationship.
5. Obtain the current 2026-09-17 Forge/Stage archives before asserting this
   older snapshot represents the current worktree. Compare source hashes and
   adoption records.
