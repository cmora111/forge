## Reconciliation of FD-0035's three open dependencies

### D1 — Freeze procedure authorization relationship

**Original requirement:** Identify legitimately applicable governance and an
independently establishable authorization relationship for a particular
subordinate Governing Freeze Procedure.

**Verified:** FD-0035 distinguishes authorization of an operation from
procedure authorization; it establishes minimum conditions and explicitly
does not authorize any particular Freeze procedure. FD-0036 and FD-0037
establish bounded constitutive legitimacy and continuing CMSA but expressly
do not authorize subordinate procedures.

**Later lead:** A reports bounded derivative capacity; B–E report act,
representation, and evidentiary requirements. These are reconstructed in the
reconciliation inventory, not verified original findings or inspections.

**Disposition:** OPEN. Even if capacity is newly established, no particular
Freeze procedure, authorization act, or operation-specific applicability is
established here. CDA authorization would also require its own determinate act;
it cannot substitute for Freeze authorization.

### D2 — Bounded stewardship scope

**Original requirement:** Establish whether CMSA's already constituted scope
includes authorizing subordinate normative procedures without expanding
higher governance, and independently evidence that scope.

**Verified:** FD-0037 establishes bounded continuing CMSA with canonical
artifacts and controlled evolution within existing authority; it explicitly
reserves subordinate-procedure-authorizing scope. FD-0036 explicitly does not
establish that scope. FD-0035 itself says CMSA standing is not automatically
procedure-authorizing standing.

**Later lead:** A reports a derivative-capacity derivation and dedicated Anvil
silence. C–E report limitations on direct exercise, form, and representation;
F reports present-holder continuity. Original complete workpieces and Anvil
outputs have not been recovered.

**Disposition:** OPEN, with a well-defined candidate derivation. The accepted
foundations plus the reconstructed A claim cannot be silently treated as a
verified authority grant. Either recover original A–F and inspection evidence
or produce a new prospective derivation and complete Anvil inspection.

### D3 — Stage 3 applicability

**Original requirement:** Establish whether an authorized Freeze procedure
could evaluate preserved Stage 3 legacy evidence without manufacturing
historical evidence or interpretive standing.

**Verified:** FD-0035 reserves this explicitly; FD-0034's interpretive boundary
is cited by FD-0035. A–F summaries address governance capacity and act form,
not a complete Stage 3 evidence-to-requirement mapping.

**Disposition:** OPEN and separate from D2. No present-stage disposition,
Freeze completion, or retrospective validation follows from this workpiece.

## Additional distinction exposed by CDA

Canonical artifact inclusion, substantive Stage review, and authorization of
CDA as a governing Discovery Acceptance procedure are different relationships.

procedure. A generic CMSA power over canonical artifacts cannot be silently
used as an authorization act. CDA's Stage review and any applicable approval
governance remain separately unverified.
