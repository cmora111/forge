# Methodological Provenance

This directory preserves non-normative historical evidence that materially
establishes the provenance of the Forge–Stage Method's engineering concepts,
dependencies, decisions, transitions, or development.

Material preserved here is historical evidence.

Preservation in this directory does not confer normative authority, canonical
standing, acceptance, procedural authority, engineering authority, or discovery
status upon the preserved material.

Historical statements preserved here may be incomplete, provisional, mistaken,
or later superseded. Their preservation establishes provenance of development,
not correctness.

Source evidence SHOULD be preserved distinctly from later interpretation or
summary so that the historical source remains inspectable.

Repository custody of an artifact in this directory does not by itself make the
artifact authoritative.

This directory does not establish a historical-artifact lifecycle, numbering
scheme, admission process, or requirement to preserve all historical material.

Its responsibility is limited to preserving methodological provenance when that
provenance is materially relevant.
